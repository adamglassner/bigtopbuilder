# Changelog

All notable changes to Big Top Builder Classic are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH). Versions before 1.0.0 are the free demo.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-11-11

### Changed
- The game is now called Big Top Builder Classic, on the title screen, the window, the top bar, the installer and its shortcuts. Your saves, settings and achievements carry over.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- Paths. Pick Path from Extras ($15 a tile), then click and drag across the grounds to lay them. Paths join up with each other, the gate and the entrances. Guests take them whenever they can, walk faster on them and stay out of the mud when it rains. Drag over a path to pick it up again for a full refund, and anything built on a path takes it up.
- Endless nights: the circus no longer packs up after three nights. Keep it running in Brookhaven as long as you like; the run only ends if you run out of money. The title screen shows your longest run.
- New achievement: Held Over, for keeping the circus running for ten nights. Opening Week (formerly Roll Out) now unlocks after your third night.

### Removed
- The Demo Complete screen after the third night.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-11

### Changed
- Softer lighting at night. Light poles, tents, carts and the gate glow much more gently, the strings of bulbs are dimmer, and the spotlights over the tents are fainter, so the grounds are no longer too bright after dark.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- The first playable demo of Big Top Builder: opening week, three nights in Brookhaven.
- Build on a 24 x 14 circus grounds: the Ring Tent and the Big Top; the Funhouse, Hall of Mirrors and Fortune Teller sideshows; Popcorn, Cotton Candy, Hot Dogs and Soda Pop carts; benches, trash cans and light poles. The Three-Ring Big Top and the Human Cannonball are shown as coming in the full game.
- The troupe: start with a clown and a juggler, and hire clowns, stilt walkers, jugglers, strongwomen, acrobats, fire breathers and trapeze artists from a noticeboard that changes every morning. Each performer has a name, a skill rating and a nightly wage.
- Show planning: book acts into each tent's lineup (three in a Ring Tent, five in the Big Top) and set the running order. Shows are rated out of five stars: open with Laughs, mix up the acts, and close with Thrills for a big finish. The trapeze only fits in the Big Top.
- Showtime from 6 to 11 PM, with pause and three speeds. Tents wait for an audience, then put on the show act by act, with the crowd's excitement shown over the tent, spotlights in the sky and applause at the end.
- Every show tires the acts out, and tired acts can flub. Everyone is rested by morning.
- Guests with names, money, hunger, thirst and energy who walk the grounds, watch shows, visit sideshows, eat, drop litter, think out loud and go home happy (or not). Kids love the clowns and take home souvenir balloons.
- Ticket prices for every tent, sideshow and cart, and gate admission. Reputation (out of five stars) sets the size of the next night's crowd.
- A results screen after every night and a summary when the tents come down after the third.
- Eleven achievements, which also show in Ironite Launcher.
- An original circus march and sound effects, all made by Ironite. Music toggles with M, full screen with F11.
- Settings and records saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\BigTopBuilder.
