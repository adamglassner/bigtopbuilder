An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
=========================================

Open Big Top Builder from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop,
or Ironite Launcher. Or just double-click BigTopBuilder.exe.

THE DEMO
You run a circus in Brookhaven. Keep it going for as many nights as you
like: the run only ends if you run out of money. The full game takes you
on tour.

HOW TO PLAY
- Daytime: pick a tent, sideshow, food cart or extra from the bar at the
  bottom and click the grounds to build it. Guests line up at the gold
  square in front of it.
- Paths: pick Path from Extras, then click and drag to lay them. Guests
  prefer paths, walk faster on them and stay out of the mud. Drag over a
  path to pick it up again.
- Troupe: open the Troupe to hire performers. You start with a clown and
  a juggler. New performers look for work every morning.
- Shows: click a tent and book acts into its lineup. Open with Laughs,
  don't put two acts of the same kind back to back, and close with
  Thrills for a big finish. The trapeze only fits in the Big Top.
- Showtime: press OPEN THE GATES. From 6 to 11 PM guests pay admission,
  watch the shows, visit sideshows, eat and spend. Space pauses; 1, 2
  and 3 set the speed.
- Every show tires the acts out, and tired acts can flub. Swap them out
  during the night. Everyone is rested by morning.
- Keep guests happy: food and drink for hunger and thirst, benches for
  tired feet, light poles for dark corners, and trash cans to keep the
  litter down. Happy guests bring bigger crowds.
- Esc = cancel / menu, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen,
  Delete = sell what's selected.

Your settings and records are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\BigTopBuilder,
outside the install folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.
